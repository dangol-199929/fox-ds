# Returns and refunds policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

## Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

## Change of mind

**DECISION:** choose one and delete the other.

- **Option A (no change-of-mind returns):** We don't offer refunds or exchanges if you simply change your mind, so please check sizes and details before ordering. This doesn't affect your rights if an item is faulty, damaged or not as described.
- **Option B (change-of-mind returns):** You can return unused items in their original packaging within **[30] days** of delivery for a refund of the item price. Return postage is paid by you, and the original shipping cost isn't refunded. Email **[EMAIL]** first for the return address. Because our suppliers are overseas, we'll give you a local return address where possible.

## Cancelling an order

Email **[EMAIL]** as soon as possible. If we haven't yet sent the order to our supplier, we'll cancel it and refund you in full. Once it's with the supplier we may not be able to stop it. If so, our change-of-mind terms above apply when it arrives.

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Shopify, usually within **[5–10] business days** of approval, depending on your bank.
