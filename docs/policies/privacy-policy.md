# Privacy policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

[BUSINESS NAME] ("we", "us") runs the Clean Desk Kit online store at [WEBSITE]. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with and your choices. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

## What we collect

- **When you order:** your name, email, phone number (if given), delivery and billing address, and what you bought. Card details are entered on Shopify's secure checkout and handled by Shopify's payment provider. **We never see or store your full card number.**
- **When you create an account:** your email address, and your name if you add it. Sign-in uses a one-time code sent to your email, so we don't store a password.
- **When you sign up to our newsletter:** your email address and your consent to receive marketing emails.
- **When you contact us:** what you send us.
- **Technical information:** our website uses a small number of essential cookies, to keep your shopping cart and to keep you signed in. Our hosting and store providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the service running. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. _[Update this if you add analytics, pixels or ads later.]_

## Why we use it

To process and deliver your order, send order and shipping updates, provide customer service and handle returns, run your account, send newsletters you've agreed to, prevent fraud, and meet our legal and tax obligations.

## Who we share it with

We only share what's needed for each purpose:

- **Shopify**, which runs our store, checkout, payments, customer accounts and emails.
- **Our suppliers and their delivery partners**, to send your order. They receive your name, delivery address, phone number and the items ordered. **Our suppliers are located overseas, mainly in China**, so this information is sent outside Australia. We only give them what's needed to deliver your order.
- **Order management tools** we use to pass orders to suppliers (DSers).
- **Vercel**, which hosts our website.
- Professional advisers and authorities, where the law requires it.

We don't sell your personal information.

## Marketing emails

We only send marketing emails if you've opted in. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or you can email us to be removed.

## Keeping it secure

Your information is stored by our service providers on secure systems. Our website runs over HTTPS, and sign-in sessions are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete your account, by emailing **[EMAIL]**. We may need to keep some order records for tax and legal reasons. If you're not happy with how we've handled a privacy issue, contact us first. If we can't resolve it, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
