# Shipping policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

## Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses in **Australia** only.

## Shipping costs

- **Free standard shipping** on orders of **$60 or more** (after any discounts).
- **$7.25 flat rate** on orders under $60, wherever you are in Australia.

All prices are in Australian dollars. The shipping cost is shown at checkout before you pay.

## Where your order ships from

Our products are made and stocked by our suppliers, and most orders are sent **directly from our suppliers' warehouses overseas** to your address. That's how we keep prices down. It does mean delivery takes longer than from an Australian warehouse, and an order with several items may arrive in more than one parcel, on different days.

## Tracking

When your order is sent, we'll email you a tracking number if one is available. Tracking can take a few days to show movement, particularly before the parcel reaches Australia.

## Customs and import charges

If you're ever asked to pay an import or customs charge on delivery, please contact us before paying it.

## Lost, late or damaged parcels

If your tracking hasn't updated for **[10] business days**, or your parcel arrives damaged, please contact us at **[EMAIL]** with your order number (and photos, if it's damaged). We'll follow it up with the supplier and carrier and arrange a replacement or refund where appropriate. See our [Refund policy](/refunds). Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law always apply.

## Wrong address

Please check your address at checkout. If you notice a mistake, contact us as soon as possible. We can usually fix it if the order hasn't been sent to our supplier yet.
